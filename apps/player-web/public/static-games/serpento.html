Serpento

Eat, grow, and don't bite your own tail.

Eat to grow, don't hit the walls (or yourself). Our take on the classic snake game. Free, no ads.

Play Serpento now. It's free, with no ads and nothing to download: it runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers.

How to play

Eat apples to grow longer and score; don't hit the walls or your own tail.

Swipe up, down, left or right to steer, or tap the arrow pad under the board. Arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard.

Harvey's note

Snakes are cool. Eat everything you can, but don't bite your own tail!

Made by James, Tilly & Harvey, a dad and his kids who make little games together.
