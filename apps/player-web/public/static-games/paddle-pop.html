Paddle Pop

Bounce the ball, smash the targets, grab power-ups.

Bounce the ball, hit the targets, grab the power-ups. A free paddle game, no ads.

Play Paddle Pop now. It's free, with no ads and nothing to download: it runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers.

How to play

Keep the balls bouncing off your paddle and bump the numbered discs for points. Don't let a fireball touch your paddle.

Hold the left or right side of the screen to slide the paddle (arrow keys or A/D on a keyboard). Hit power-ups with the ball to collect them.

Harvey's note

Don't let the marble fall! Grab the power-ups, they're the best bit.

Made by James, Tilly & Harvey, a dad and his kids who make little games together.
